# Demo video script (target 1:50, hard limit 2:00)

Rules say the video "should be less than two minutes". Record on the phone
with the built-in screen recorder. Voice-over can be added afterwards.

## Before you press record
1. Open https://techinews-api.onrender.com/health in the phone's browser and
   confirm it loads instantly. If it takes more than 2 seconds, wait a minute
   and try again. Never record a cold start.
2. Turn on Do Not Disturb so nothing personal appears on screen.
3. Sign out of Pro state: Account, Reset this device, so the paywall and the
   unlock both happen on camera.
4. Save nine stories beforehand, so the tenth and eleventh saves happen on camera.
5. Do three full takes. Pick the best. Do not edit a bad take.

## Notes
- Say "Test Store" out loud. Judges know that dialog; naming it reads as
  understanding, hiding it reads as unfinished.
- Do not claim users, downloads, or "production ready".
- If the push row is skipped, do not mention instant alerts as working. Say
  "Pro unlocks instant alerts" and show the setting only.
