# Privacy

*Draft. Review before publishing to a store.*

**What TechiNews stores on your device:** your chosen topics, notification preference, saved and read story ids, and cached copies of saved stories. This never leaves the device unless you sign in.

**If you sign in with Google:** we store your name, email, and profile photo URL from Google, your topics and notification preference, and a push-notification token so digests can reach you. Nothing else. We do not sell or share this data.

**What we do not do:** no advertising trackers, no analytics that identify you, no reading of your contacts or location.

**The news itself:** every story links to its original publisher. Summaries are generated by a language model from the publisher's public page and may contain errors; the source link is always one tap away.

**Deleting your data:** sign out and choose "Reset this device" to clear local data. To delete a signed-in account, email ai@globalvoxinc.com from the address you signed in with.
